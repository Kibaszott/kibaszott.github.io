Work in review.

16 August 2026

Amost three weeks ago, when I first created this site, I had just begun my first full-time position in over three years. Prior to this, while studying, I had held positions in both the private and public sectors working in vary capacities as a development engineer, consultant and designer.

It was my previous job which I had that I thought that I was comfortable, that I could go into the office day after day and complete the tasks and go home. At the time, this assuredness wasn't comforting; it made me uneasy to think that I would be relinquishing opportunity for familiarity, that without discomfort, resistance and struggle, I would stagnate or, even worse, regress.
It was, I guess, in a sense fortuitous that the company which I had worked at for a total of four and a half years closed its doors, giving me no option but to step outside of my comfort.

NOw, it is not lost on me that the current feelings which overwhelm me — the paralysing fear of inadequacy, the doubt and shame which prickle beneth my skin, the desperate need to be perfect, be a valuable member of the team — all these concerns, revolting feelings and desires are the primary antitheses to my fear of comfort.
... And yet! And yet I feel no better. My discontent has shifted, bubbled up from my stomach into my throat, I can taste it. I hope I improve, I hope I become happy. This isn't forever, but while I'm there, I still want to be the best of myself.
