Work in review.

16 August 2026

Amost three weeks ago, when I first created this site, I had just begun at my first full-time job in over three years. Prior to this, while studying, I had held positions in both the private and public sectors working in varying capacities as a development engineer, consultant and designer.

It was my previous role which I had that I thought that I was comfortable, that I could go into the office day after day and complete the tasks and go home. However, at the time, this assuredness wasn't comforting; it made me uneasy to think that I would be relinquishing opportunity for familiarity, that without discomfort, resistance and struggle, I would stagnate or, even worse, regress.
It was, I guess, in a sense fortuitous that the company which I had worked at for a total of four and a half years closed its doors, giving me no option but to step outside of this haven.

Now, it is not lost on me that the current feelings which I am overwhelmed by — the paralysing fear of inadequacy, the doubt and shame which prickle beneath my skin, the desperate need to be perfect or at the very least be deemed adequate, the wanting be a valuable member of the team — all these concerns, revolting feelings and desires are the primary antitheses to my fear of comfort.
... And yet! And yet I feel no better. My discontent has shifted, bubbled up from my stomach into my throat, I can taste it. I hope I improve, I hope I become happy. This isn't forever, but while I'm there, I still want to be the best of myself.
